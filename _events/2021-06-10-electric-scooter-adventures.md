Come join us as we find some electric scooters and practice on them! This is a great opportunity for riders of all experience levels.

If you're new to riding, we'll help you get comfortable on an electric scooter. If you're already an experienced rider, come hang out and socialize with the group.

Everyone is welcome regardless of experience level. Safety is our priority, so we'll make sure everyone feels comfortable before hitting the road.
