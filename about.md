  <h4>Our Mission</h4>
  <p>The mission of the Sparkling Pink Pandas group is to promote inclusivity and safety for trans individuals in society. We strive to create a welcoming and supportive environment where all trans, intersex, and gender-variant people can come together and be celebrated for who they are.</p>
  <p>We seek to promote greater awareness and understanding of gender diversity in society. We believe that education and advocacy are crucial in creating a more accepting and inclusive world, and we strive to be a voice for the trans community.</p>
  <p>We believe that to do this we need to help improve access to safety gear for trans folks. We believe that everyone deserves to ride safely and comfortably, and we work to ensure that trans riders have the resources they need to do so.</p>
  <p>Through community events, group rides, and outreach efforts, we aim to create a space where trans individuals feel supported and valued. We believe that by working together, we can create a more inclusive and just society where everyone can live their lives to the fullest.</p>

  <h4>FAQ</h4>

  <p><strong>Who can join the Sparkling Pink Pandas?</strong></p>
  <p>We welcome all queer and femme-ish folks -- in whatever way feels meaningful to you. If you identify with our community and want to ride, you're welcome here. Trans, nonbinary, intersex, gender-variant, and all allies who share our values are invited.</p>

  <p><strong>Do I need a Vespa or scooter to ride with you?</strong></p>
  <p>Not at all! We ride everything from 49cc scooters to 1200cc motorcycles and everything in between. If it has two wheels (or three!) and gets you moving, bring it. We've had riders on rental scooters, mopeds, vintage Vespas, sport bikes, and big touring motorcycles. The vibe matters more than the cc's.</p>

  <p><strong>I'm new to riding. Can I still join?</strong></p>
  <p>Absolutely! We love helping new riders get comfortable. Reach out to us so we can recommend a good practice ride for you -- we do group practice rides, make sure nobody gets left behind, and prioritize safety. Everyone starts somewhere.</p>

  <p><strong>Where do you ride?</strong></p>
  <p>We're based in San Francisco and ride all over the Bay Area. Past rides have taken us from the Mission to Pacifica for tacos, through the city for Slurpees, up to the North Bay, and everywhere in between. We also ride in the SF Pride Parade with Dykes on Bikes every year.</p>

  <p><strong>How do I find out about rides?</strong></p>
  <p>Join our <a href="https://groups.google.com/g/SparklingPinkPandas">Google Group</a> for ride announcements, follow us on <a href="https://www.instagram.com/sparklingpinkpandas/">Instagram</a> and <a href="https://x.com/spp_scooters">X / Twitter</a>, or subscribe to our <a href="/events.ics">events calendar</a>.</p>
